This privacy policy describes how Day Trader Tycoon ("the App") handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Most of your game progress — your portfolio, settings, and history — is saved locally on your device using your device's local storage and never leaves it. The only information the App sends to an external server is the Global Leaderboard data described below.

Global Leaderboard

The App includes an optional Global Leaderboard. Participation is opt-in only: nothing is sent to the leaderboard unless you explicitly choose to join when prompted in the game, and you can stop sharing at any time using the Leaderboard toggle in the in-game Shop. If you opt in, the App periodically sends the following information to our database, hosted by Supabase, a third-party backend provider:

  • Firm name — the name you've chosen for your firm in-game. You can change this at any time, and we recommend not using your real name.
  • Score and prestige count — your all-time earnings and number of IPOs (prestiges), used to rank your position on the leaderboard.
  • A randomly generated player ID — a string created on your device the first time you play (e.g. p_a1b2c3d4...). This ID is not derived from and is not linked to your name, Apple ID, device identifiers, or any other personal information. It exists solely so the leaderboard can recognize and update your existing entry.

No account, sign-in, or personal information is required to appear on the leaderboard. Leaderboard data is visible to anyone who opens the Leaderboard tab.

Opting out via the Shop toggle stops all future submissions; your most recent entry may remain visible on the board. If you'd like your leaderboard entry removed, contact us (below) with your firm name and approximate score, and we will remove it manually.

Supabase's own privacy policy is available at https://supabase.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS, subject to your device's tracking permission settings)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App will request your permission before allowing AdMob to access your device's advertising identifier, in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You may deny this permission and the App will continue to function normally — you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App does not collect or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all purchase transactions.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Data Retention

Game save data is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or use the in-game "Liquidate Everything" reset option.

Global Leaderboard entries (firm name, score, prestige count, and player ID — see above) are retained on our servers indefinitely unless you request removal as described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
