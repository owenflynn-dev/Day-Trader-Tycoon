Think you can beat Wall Street? Prove it.

Day Trader Tycoon is an addictive idle clicker where you build a trading empire from a $4 Penny Stock position to a multi-trillion dollar Global Reserve Currency. Hire traders, unlock markets, and watch your firm print money — even while you sleep.

IDLE MECHANICS THAT ACTUALLY WORK
