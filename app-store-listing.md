## Description (4000 chars max)

Think you can beat Wall Street? Prove it.

Day Trader Tycoon is an addictive idle clicker where you build a trading empire from a $4 Penny Stock position to a multi-trillion dollar Global Reserve Currency. Hire traders, unlock markets, and watch your firm print money — even while you sleep.

**BUILD YOUR EMPIRE**
Start with Penny Stocks and grind your way up through Meme Coins, AI Bubble Co., Hedge Funds, Private Equity, and beyond. Each asset automates with a hired trader, compounding your passive income while you focus on the next opportunity.

**SURVIVE THE MARKET**
Random market events keep you on your toes — Fed rate cuts, WSB pump schemes, Black Swan crashes, and SEC investigations. React fast to cash in on the upside. Hedge against the downside. Or just watch the chaos unfold.

**MULTIPLE MARKETS**
Conquer Wall Street, then unlock the Tokyo Trading Floor and London Stock Exchange. Each market has unique assets, currencies, and events. Go global or go broke.

**DEEP STRATEGY**
- IPO your firm to bank Influence and unlock permanent multipliers
- Hire premium managers — from HFT algorithms to inside-info Senators
- Climb the Skill Tree for passive bonuses
- Master the Chicago Pit for quick credits
- Play the Options Floor with real CALL/PUT mechanics and leverage
- Survive Black Swan events for permanent +25% bonuses

**IDLE MECHANICS THAT ACTUALLY WORK**
Your traders keep working when the app is closed. Come back to a pile of cash, a market event already in progress, and a Daily Quest waiting to be claimed.

**NO PAY-TO-WIN**
Everything in the game can be earned through play. Optional IAPs let you accelerate the grind — nothing is locked behind a paywall.
